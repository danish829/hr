Attendance & Punctuality Review

July–September 2026. Biometric in/out records, 121 staff, 5,765 present person-days. An arrival counts as late when the first punch is after 10:10 am. Scope: mapped business verticals only — Management, Support, unmapped and test accounts excluded.

More than 1 in 3 workdays began after 10:10 am, holding steady across all three months. Most staff arrive between 9:30 and 10:00, so the norm is achievable; when people are late, they are late by 48 minutes on average.
